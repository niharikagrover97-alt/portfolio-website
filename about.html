about me

Hi, I’m Niharika.

I’m a UX researcher and designer with a background in fine art — which means I was trained to observe carefully long before I learned to run a study.

Art school teaches you a particular discipline: look at what is actually in front of you, not what you assume is there. Hold judgment until you’ve really seen the thing. That habit translated directly into research — noticing the workaround a user doesn’t mention, the hesitation before a click, the gap between what people say and what they do.
